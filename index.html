About me

Hello! I'm Paulo, an engineer with a strong background in geospatial technology and data science. I hold a Bachelor's degree in Geoinformation and Geomatics Engineering from the Polytechnic University of Catalonia, which I completed in July 2023.

Currently, I work as an Engineering Technologist at the Centre Tecnològic de Telecomunicacions de Catalunya (CTTC), where I support the Geomatics unit by developing software solutions and implementing machine and deep learning algorithms to perform advanced data analysis.

My previous experience includes working at the Institut Cartogràfic i Geològic de Catalunya (ICGC), where I developed task automation tools, data analysis software, and managed GNSS positioning systems for the Geodesy service. I also served as a research assistant in Planetary Science at Constructor University in Bremen, Germany, contributing to the Earth-Server2 project by developing software for image processing and ice classification on Mars.

Since September 2023, I am pursuing a Master's degree in Computer Vision, a joint program offered by several universities including Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona, Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya, Universitat Pompeu Fabra, Universitat Oberta de Catalunya, and Universitat de Barcelona. This program allows me to deepen my expertise in applying computer vision techniques to geospatial data and beyond.
